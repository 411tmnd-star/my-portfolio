The planned central orchestration layer for contextual memory, multi-model routing (OpenAI, Anthropic, open-source), user state, and task execution. Designed with Nigerian data-protection requirements in mind; formal compliance depends on the final system, data flows, and deployment context.

[PLANNED ARCHITECTURE · LOCALIZED VOICE AI]

411 Voice — Nigerian-Language Speech

A speech layer aimed at natural spoken interaction in Igbo, Hausa, and Yoruba alongside English and Pidgin — so more Nigerians can use AI in the language they actually speak at home, not just English. Early-stage direction, built on the speaker-verification groundwork from the VoiceAnalysis project. No dedicated AI law exists in Nigeria yet, though the proposed National Digital Economy and E-Governance Bill would introduce a risk-based AI framework once passed — this is being tracked and designed for, not claimed as compliant today.
Igbo / Hausa / Yoruba Speech AI Regulatory-Aware Design
