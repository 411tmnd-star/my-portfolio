⚡ Concept & Prototype Architecture

VoltaEdge AI — Physical Intelligence for Unreliable Energy Networks

An edge-computing energy management platform engineered to monitor electricity consumption, classify appliance-level usage via TinyML, and protect sensitive hardware across Nigerian grid, generator, and solar installations.

01 / Problem Space

Electricity in Nigeria is an economic and protection challenge

Managing power requires constant manual intervention—balancing prepaid token depletion, generator fuel usage, unstable grid voltages, and unmonitored heavy loads.

⚠️ Blind Token Depletion
Prepaid meters run out abruptly because users lack real-time visibility into which appliance is burning through units.
⚡ Voltage Instability
Surges, sag, and brownouts during grid-to-generator switchovers frequently damage expensive compressors and electronics.
🔌 Multi-Source Complexity
Managing load priorities between Solar Inverters, Public Grid (DisCo), and Petrol/Diesel Generators is entirely unautomated.

04 / Edge ML Architecture

Non-Intrusive Load Monitoring (NILM)

Instead of placing a smart plug on every appliance, VoltaEdge samples aggregate current waveforms at the main distribution board and uses TinyML feature extraction to identify active appliances.

NILM Execution Pipeline

High-frequency sampling (1–10 kHz) → Active/Reactive power signature extraction → Harmonics analysis → On-device TinyML classification → Appliance state identified.

Electrical Safety & Protection Considerations ▼

Engineering Verification Notice: Production readiness requires formal compliance testing under applicable standards. Key design areas under evaluation:

  • Creepage and clearance distances matched to working voltages and pollution degrees.
  • Transient surge protection using matched Metal Oxide Varistors (MOVs) and Thermal Fuses.
  • Contactor/Relay selection matched to inductive motor startup loads (AC-3 rating).
